# Originality Checklist

## Author contribution
- [ ] Distinctive author contribution present: experience, experiment,
      implementation, unique observation, original example, architecture,
      opinion, lesson learned, comparison, or research synthesis
- [ ] The article offers something the top search results don't

## If source material was used
- [ ] No near-verbatim passages (quoted text is quoted and attributed)
- [ ] No close paraphrase / synonym-swapping ("mosaic plagiarism")
- [ ] Section structure is independent — not the source's outline reused
- [ ] Distinctive examples/scenarios from the source are NOT reused without
      attribution (better: replaced with your own)
- [ ] Source's data/quotes/images/code attributed; licenses respected
- [ ] Source's claims independently verified (fact-check workflow)
- [ ] The article stands on its own without the source

## Attribution
- [ ] Every non-obvious fact has a citation at the point of use
- [ ] Primary sources cited (not just blogs citing them)
- [ ] Quotes exact and attributed
- [ ] Code snippets: license checked, attribution included
- [ ] Images: owned, licensed, or credited (Unsplash etc.)

## AI usage honesty
- [ ] AI usage matches Medium's disclosure rules (see the medium reference)
- [ ] No AI-generated text presented as personal experience
- [ ] No AI-hallucinated facts, statistics, or citations
