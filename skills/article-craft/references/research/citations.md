# Citations (reference)

> Article Craft working standard (INDUSTRY_BEST_PRACTICE), with Medium
> policy context where noted.

## Principles

1. **Cite facts you did not experience.** If you didn't measure it, run it,
   or live it, name who did.
2. **Cite the primary source.** Link to the documentation, the paper, the
   changelog — not to a blog that summarizes it, when the primary is
   reachable.
3. **Quote sparingly and exactly.** Quote only what you're analyzing;
   paraphrase (with attribution) the rest — in your own structure, not the
   source's (see the originality guard).
4. **Cite at the point of use**, not in one footnote pile at the end.
5. **Date-protect technical claims**: "as of Python 3.12" beats an uncited
   present-tense claim that will silently rot.

## Mechanics on Medium

Medium's Plagiarism Guidelines note citations "can be added using numbered
superscript text formatting, or other appropriate methods"
(OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT). Practical options:

- Inline links on the claim itself (most common on Medium).
- Numbered footnote list at the end with inline superscript-style markers.
- A short "Sources" section for technical tutorials, listing docs and
  versions used.

## What not to do

- Do not cite a source you did not actually read.
- Do not cite AI chat output as a source; cite what it cited, after checking.
- Do not use citations as SEO link-farm padding (Medium Rules prohibit
  link-farming; OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT).
- Do not present an affiliate link as a citation (FTC disclosure required;
  OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT).
