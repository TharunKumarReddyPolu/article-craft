# Medium Distribution Guidelines (reference)

> Source: [Medium's Distribution Guidelines](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution)
> (official, updated 2026-06-29; verified 2026-09-06). See `sources.yaml` → `medium-distribution-guidelines`.

Medium assigns every story to one of three distribution tiers:

| Tier | What it means |
|---|---|
| Network | Baseline: shown to followers of the writer and publication. |
| General | Also matched to readers by topic interests and related writers. |
| Boost | Especially high-quality stories, promoted more heavily across Medium. |

A human curation team reviews stories seven days a week. **Boost is
descriptive, not a formula** — Medium states explicitly that the criteria
"should not be interpreted as a checklist of components that guarantee Boost."

## What curators look for (OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT for Boost consideration)

1. **Writer's experience** — a clear reason why *this* writer writes about
   *this* topic; credible first-hand experience; insights integral to the
   story. The story must be human-created and not appear AI-generated.
2. **Value and impact** — the reader's life is enriched; time well spent.
3. **Respect for the reader** — not a sales pitch or a bid for attention.
4. **Non-derivative** — a fresh perspective, not a paraphrase, recombination,
   or rehash of easily found information.
5. **Writing and craftsmanship** — well-written, free of errors, appropriately
   sourced, narratively strong; length appropriate to purpose; images that add
   value with ALT text and credits; correct formatting.

## What disqualifies a story from General Distribution (OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT)

- **Clickbait** title, subtitle, or cover image (misleading, dishonest,
  sensational).
- **Unconstructive negativity**: outrage porn, misery lit, trolling, private
  disputes, rants without insight.
- **Low-value content**, including: AI-generated content; crypto airdrop
  announcements; sponsored content / content marketing / PR; reputation
  laundering; stories whose primary point is signups/traffic/sales/donations;
  unoriginal, derivative, or generic content; summaries of content from other
  sources; link round-ups; link-farming; writing too poor to comprehend.
- **NSFW content** (erotica, graphic violence, prominent profanity).
- **Misinformation** (Medium curation does not fact-check, but recognized
  misinformation is ineligible).
- **Topic/mentions spamming** (tags unrelated to content; mass mentions).
- **Rules violations** (e.g., copyrighted images without permission,
  unverified claims that could cause harm — including health claims).

## Notable specific rules (OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT)

- Stories about the Partner Program, making money on Medium, or Boost itself
  are set to Network Distribution only. Writers are asked to tag these
  `medium-meta`.
- Republished **translations** of other sources' work are not Boost-eligible.
- Syndicating **your own** work from your blog is fine (see
  `canonical-links.md`).
- Only English stories are reviewed for Boost/General at this time.

## What Article Craft does with this (HEURISTIC)

Article Craft checks drafts against the disqualifier list and the Boost
characteristics and reports findings labeled `POLICY` (from this page),
`RECOMMENDATION` (official-sourced advice), or `HEURISTIC` (Article Craft's
editorial judgment). Article Craft **never** predicts distribution or claims
any check guarantees a Boost.
