# Medium Titles & Subtitles (reference)

> Source: [Distribution Guidelines](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution)
> (official, updated 2026-06-29; verified 2026-09-06). See `sources.yaml`.

## Official guidance (OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT)

From the Distribution Guidelines (disqualifiers for Boost / General):

> "Titles, subtitles, and cover image (if any) should not be sensationalistic
> or have the feel of a screaming tabloid headline. Being overly generic,
> mysterious, or formulaic is just as bad as being sensationalistic. In both
> cases, the story doesn't give the reader a good idea of what they'll be
> clicking into."

> "We will not Boost a story if we don't believe the title, subtitle, and
> cover image provide enough context about the story to appeal to readers in
> the feed."

Practical reading (OFFICIAL_RECOMMENDATION):

- A clickbait title/subtitle **disqualifies** a story from General
  Distribution, not just Boost.
- The title/subtitle pair is what readers see when deciding to read; it must
  represent the story accurately.

## What Article Craft does with this (HEURISTIC)

The title system (`article-craft new`, `review`) generates candidates across
approaches — descriptive, problem-oriented, outcome-oriented, curiosity-driven,
technical, beginner-friendly, experience-based — and scores each on clarity,
specificity, reader promise, accuracy, and clickbait risk. Hard rules:

- Never recommend a title that misrepresents the article's content.
- Flag ALL-CAPS words, naked numbers-plus-superlatives ("7 SHOCKING..."),
  "you won't believe", "this one trick", "die", "insane", "hackers hate",
  miracle claims, and similar tabloid patterns as clickbait risk.
- Flag vacuous titles ("Some Thoughts on X", "Everything You Need to Know
  About X" when the article is an intro) as generic risk.
- Prefer title + subtitle combos where the subtitle adds the promise, the
  angle, or the scope the title can't fit.
