# Medium Topics (reference)

> Sources: [Writing and publishing your first story](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/225168768-Writing-and-publishing-your-first-story)
> and [Distribution Guidelines](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution)
> (official; verified 2026-09-06). See `sources.yaml`.

## Official guidance (OFFICIAL_REQUIREMENT / OFFICIAL_RECOMMENDATION)

- At publish time you can add topics (tags) to your story from the publish
  menu.
- **Topic/mentions spamming** disqualifies a story from General
  Distribution: tagging topics inappropriately to spam those topics' readers
  (Medium's example: a dating story tagged "technology").
- Large numbers of mentions of other users (e.g., tagging 20 users) likewise
  disqualify from General Distribution.
- Stories about Medium itself should be tagged `medium-meta`.

## What Article Craft does with this (HEURISTIC)

- Suggests topic candidates from the article's actual content and stated
  audience; every suggestion must be grounded in a topic the article
  substantively covers.
- Flags suspected topic-stuffing: tags not supported by article content
  (a keyword that appears once in the intro does not justify a tag).
- Flags excessive @mentions (V1 heuristic: more than ~3 mentions in body
  text).
