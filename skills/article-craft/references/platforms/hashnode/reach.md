# Reach: Hashnode's Official Discoverability Mechanics

What Hashnode officially documents about how articles get found — summarized
from Hashnode's own support documentation so the adapter checks alignment
with documented mechanics, not folklore.

Sources: `hashnode-tags`, `hashnode-seo`, `hashnode-write-article`,
`hashnode-conduct` (verified 2026-09-07). Hashnode publishes no boost
program; reach follows from documented mechanics: tags, SEO fields, and the
community graph.

## Official mechanics

- **Tags** — articles are discovered through Hashnode's tag system; tags
  are selected from Hashnode's tag list in the editor (hashnode-tags). No
  official maximum is documented, and the adapter does not invent one.
- **Blog-level SEO fields** — hashnode-seo documents the display title,
  search description, and social image (plus sitemap and AMP toggle). The
  *search description* and *social image* are exactly what search engines
  and social cards show readers before they click.
- **Titles and slugs** — hashnode-write-article covers setting the article
  title and its URL slug; a descriptive slug keeps links meaningful when
  shared.
- **Community conduct** — hashnode-conduct is the baseline; moderation
  action is the fastest way to lose reach anywhere.
- **AI content** — Hashnode's generative-AI stance is enforced via
  moderation and the conduct baseline (the adapter's policy checks, not a
  reach mechanic).

## What the reach engine checks (and why)

Advisory signals only: tag presence/coverage, cover/social-image presence
with alt text, SEO-relevant title/description alignment, and heading
structure. None of it predicts feed placement or search ranking.
